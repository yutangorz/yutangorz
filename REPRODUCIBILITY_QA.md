# Reproducibility scope and known prerequisites

This is a scientific-method archive, not an end-to-end numerical-reproduction certificate. Static checks validate files, parsing, declared local references and saved-result arithmetic. No scientific module was imported or executed.

- Final OQMD selection has checkpoint/order dependencies; the independent initially screened branch is not a complete historical replay. The PVK classifier weight is withheld for rights review.
- Actual ALIGNN config/splits exist; loss/scheduler execution identity and a complete compatible environment remain unresolved.
- ET/XGBoost/SHAP/LIME and refitted ablation require upstream OOF/attribution products and valid input identities. Original source/hash/receipt conditions remain. Relocated source is not silently substituted into an old expected hash.
- CRISP-X requires declared development/confirmation pools and supports. High coverage does not imply separate L/Θ slope identifiability. No abstention is converted to zero.
- JARVIS structures/derived arrays require rights-cleared acquisition. The common-Ridge sampling experiment and native-method comparisons are distinct.
- VASP/PAW/LOBSTER must be obtained separately. Actual materials/protocols/coordinates are retained without claiming all original output prerequisites are included.
- Original COHP and matched-nine-state series have distinct conditions. Occupied ICOHP and moving CBM-window integrals are not interchangeable.
- Paths labelled unavailable_inputs/ identify missing original prerequisites or source references. They are not fabricated files. Detailed private old/new/source-hash mapping is retained outside the public repository. Frozen source IDs or RNG namespaces that affect reproducibility are preserved where necessary.

Public release hashes validate the current layout. They do not replace original scientific identity checks, erase unavailable prerequisites or prove physical correctness. Complete scientific execution remains conditional on those prerequisites.
